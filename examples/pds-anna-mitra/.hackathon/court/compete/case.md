# CASE — Competition Trial: Differentiation & Defensibility

## Question on Trial
Does Anna-Mitra Dual-Channel demonstrate genuine user differentiation and operational defensibility against existing PDS systems (Mera Ration app, Ahara portal, Annavitran, and manual registers), or does it merely duplicate existing government software?

## Evidence Presented
1. **Competitive Analysis (`COMPETITIVE-ANALYSIS.md`):** Benchmarked against 5 existing solutions. Acknowledges that central portals already compute statutory quotas and ePOS machines handle live transactions when connectivity is available.
2. **Specific Last-Mile Differentiation (`DIFFERENTIATION.md` & `COMPETE.md`):**
   - Channel 1 solves the *physical arrival blindspot* on basic keypad phones in spoken Kannada without requiring 4G data or app installation.
   - Channel 2 solves the *counter outage freeze* by providing HMAC-SHA256 hash-chained offline receipts that conform to state 5–10% exception limits, replacing unverified paper ledgers.
3. **Coexistence Architecture:** Defines a realistic 4-tier coexistence model (Level 0 standalone prototype → Level 2 ePOS background microservice → Level 3 directorate MoU) rather than demanding an impossible immediate rip-and-replace of certified ePOS hardware.
