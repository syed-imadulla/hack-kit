# IDEAS — Long OPD waits

> Summary: 8 ideas from 6 directions (illustrative sample) · chosen: voice check-in + doctor-approved tests while waiting (access shift) · riskiest assumption: patients can describe symptoms by voice in a noisy OPD

## Root cause
| Why? | Answer | Label |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Patients wait hours for a few minutes with the doctor | [T] |
| 2 | History-taking and tests happen only after the doctor sees them | [AI] |
| 3 | Nothing useful happens during the waiting time | [AI] |
| 4 | Front desks have no time to collect details | [T] |
| 5 | Many patients can't use digital forms | [AI] |

- **Today's workaround:** patients repeat their story to each person; many return another day just to show reports.
- **Who suffers most often:** elderly and low-literacy patients.
- **Who suffers the worst consequences:** patients with danger signs who wait unnoticed.

## Directions explored
| Direction | Idea | What makes it different |
|---|---|---|
| User shift | Nurse-first triage dashboard | Serves the nurse, not the patient |
| Process shift | Tests ordered before the consult, with doctor approval | Uses the waiting time |
| Access shift | Voice check-in by WhatsApp or a normal call, in Kannada | No app, no typing |
| Integration shift | Pull past records from ABHA | Uses an existing system |
| Prevention shift | Follow-up calls to prevent repeat visits | Acts after the visit |
| Technology shift | AI scribe for doctors | Saves doctor time, not patient time |
| Constraint removal | "No AI": a printed symptom card at the counter | Shows the AI must add real value |
| Combination | Access + process shift | Voice check-in feeds doctor-approved tests |

## Chosen idea
**Voice check-in + doctor-approved basic tests while waiting**: patients check in by voice in their own language, and basic tests start, after a doctor approves them, while they wait.
Why: no app needed · uses the waiting time · keeps the doctor in charge
Chosen by: captain · 2026-11-19 17:35 IST

## Rejected ideas (and why)
| Idea | Why not now |
|---|---|
| Kiosk check-in | Needs hardware most hospitals don't have |
| AI scribe | Already crowded; saves doctor time, not the patient's wait |
| Patient app | Least-digital users can't install or use it |
