## POV statement
First-time and low-literacy applicants need a way to find out what's wrong with their documents **before** they submit, because a rejection means starting the whole application again ([V] Odisha e-District FAQ).

## How might we…
1. How might we catch fixable document problems at the counter, in under 30 seconds?
2. How might we tell applicants what to bring *before* their first trip?
3. How might we explain a mismatch in Kannada so a non-reader understands it?
4. How might we make the operator's job easier, not longer?

## Job story
When I'm applying for a certificate I need for a scholarship, I want to know my documents are right before the operator submits them, so I don't lose another day and miss the deadline.

## Persona
**Lakshmi, 19, Tumakuru district, first-year BA student, family shares one Android phone, speaks Kannada.** Her scholarship form needs an income certificate within 3 weeks. Her father works on others' farms, so there are no salary slips. The CSC operator uploads her father's Aadhaar, ration card and a self-declaration. Ten days later the portal shows "Rejected: income proof." She has to start again, and the scholarship deadline is close. (Persona is [AI].)

## Who is left out today
People who can't read the rejection reason, and people with no salary slips (farm labourers). The idea must work through the operator, spoken in Kannada, and must handle agricultural or self-declared income documents.
