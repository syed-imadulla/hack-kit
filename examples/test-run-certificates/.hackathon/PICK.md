## Chosen problem
**S2: repeat visits for government certificates**: rejected applications must be filed again from scratch ([V] Odisha e-District FAQ), and several common rejection reasons are catchable before submission.
Why:
1. Higher score, with no red flags.
2. The jury's doubts are testable at the venue in 30 minutes (ask CSC operators about their last rejections).
3. It fits the team's AI and speech skills and the sponsor's Indian-language speech credits.
