CASE: S1 — farmers selling without price information
QUESTION ON TRIAL: Is this problem real and worth solving here, and can this team solve it for its real users in the time?
PROBLEM: Small and marginal farmers sell produce without knowing current mandi prices and demand, and get lower prices.
REAL USERS: end user = farmer with 1–2 acres · least-digital user = older farmer with a keypad phone, regional language only · operator = FPO staff or extension worker
SOLUTION (rough direction): The farmer calls a number, says crop and village by voice, and hears today's prices at nearby mandis with travel cost, plus a simple "hold or sell" suggestion.
CONSTRAINTS: 23 h left; team = backend, frontend, AI (LLM + speech), design/pitch; rubric top items Problem 25, Innovation 25, Feasibility 20; public price data (Agmarknet) can be delayed or patchy.
EVIDENCE (labelled):
  [V] A 2025 study of NSSO 77th-round data says the "majority of the smallholding farmers" face distress sale, citing limited market access, information asymmetry, financial pressures and exploitative practices; no exact percentage in the abstract (Indian Journal of Agricultural Marketing, via ideas.repec.org, opened 19 Nov 2026)
  [V] The Wire's Agmarknet analysis: farmers in 11 states lost at least Rs 1,881 crore by selling below MSP in Oct–Nov (2020); sales outside mandis likely fetch even less (via Janata Weekly, opened 19 Nov 2026)
  [AI] Debt and lack of storage often force sales regardless of price
