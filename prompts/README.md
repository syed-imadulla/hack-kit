# Copy-ready prompts

Short prompts teammates can paste into any chat when they are working in parallel and don't have the plugin.

## Decode one statement
> Decode this hackathon problem statement in plain words: the real problem, who posted it and why, the real end user, the operator, the least-digital user who could be left out, hidden constraints (language, offline, devices, existing systems, privacy), the root cause (ask why 3 times), data available, the obvious idea 70% of teams will build, a smarter angle, and what a 24 h MVP could show. Statement: <paste>

## Find evidence
> Find 5 sourced facts proving this problem is real in <country/region>: scale, severity, who suffers. Open each source, give the link and date, and mark anything you couldn't open as UNVERIFIED. Also find 3 real complaints (news, forums, app reviews) — paraphrase them. Problem: <paste>

## Competitor sweep
> List 8 existing solutions for <problem>: startups (incl. shut down ones and why), government portals, big-company pilots, research prototypes, past hackathon projects, and today's workaround. Then make a gap table on what matters to the user: works without app, voice/regional language, least-digital user, human check, fits existing system, cost to user. Use Yes/Partly/No/Not found.

## Be the domain expert
> Act as a senior <doctor/farmer/teacher/...> with 20 years of experience. Here is our hackathon idea: <paste>. Tell me 6 reasons it would fail in real life — how the work really happens, who is allowed to decide, what staff would refuse. For each, give the smallest fix that keeps the idea's core.

## Mom-Test venue questions
> Write 6 interview questions about <problem> for <user type> that ask about their past behaviour, not opinions about my idea. Keep it under 5 minutes.

## Quick trial
> Put this idea on trial. Prosecution: 3 points it does NOT solve a real problem for real users. Defense: 3 points it does. Then 6 jurors (end user, least-digital user, front-line worker, domain professional, root-cause analyst, engineer) each vote GUILTY/NOT GUILTY with confidence 1–10, reason and fix. Then a verdict, ratings /10 and top 3 fixes. No business or revenue arguments. Idea: <paste>
