## 4. Legal and Domain Accuracy

### 4.1 Statutory Decoupling (NFSA vs. State Schemes)
- **Central NFSA PMGKAY Baseline**: Sourced from Section 3(1) and Schedule I of the National Food Security Act, 2013, as extended by the Union Cabinet through 31 December 2028:
  - Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY): Fixed 35 kg foodgrain per household per month, 100% free.
  - Priority Households (PHH): Fixed 5 kg foodgrain per member per month, 100% free.
- **Karnataka 2026–27 State Policy**:
  - In lieu of procuring additional rice (under procurement shortages), Karnataka provides DBT cash transfers of ₹170/member/month for PHH cards, and subsidized Tur Dal (1 kg/month at ₹25/kg) under state budgetary allocations.
  - Sourced from Karnataka Food & Civil Supplies Department executive guidelines.
- **Audit Finding**: The prototype strictly decouples central and state benefits. Receipts print `CENTRAL NFSA FREE` in bold, legally safeguarding beneficiaries from illegal dealer handling fees.

### 4.2 ePOS Contingency & Exception Quotas
- Sourced from Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution guidelines on ePOS offline operations:
  - Offline transactions are restricted to a contingency ceiling of 5% to 10% of total monthly shop allocation.
  - Transactions exceeding this ceiling trigger automatic taluk food inspector review.
- **Audit Finding**: Modeled accurately in `STRESS-TEST.md`, `COMPETITIVE-ANALYSIS.md`, and `DEMO.md`.

### 4.3 Data Protection & Citizen Privacy
- Under Aadhaar Act 2016 Section 29, public display or unauthorized logging of core biometric data is prohibited.
- **Audit Finding**: The local SQLite database stores zero biometric data and no Aadhaar numbers (only 12-digit ration card IDs). Voice responses announce zero PII (no names, no caste, no address).

## 10. Remaining Risks & Operational Boundaries

1. **Physical Field Pilot Requirement**: The prototype proves technical viability and cryptographic integrity, but physical field validation (EXP-01, EXP-02, EXP-03) is required before deploying with live beneficiaries or licensed FPS dealers.
2. **Hardware RTC Drift**: Long-term offline operation on cheap ePOS hardware without network NTP synchronization may cause RTC drift. The system mitigates this via monotonic checks and external checkpoint comparison, but field service SOPs must periodically calibrate hardware RTC clocks.
3. **Telephony Latency in Deep Rural Areas**: Basic phone voice prompts depend on 2G GSM cellular voice channels. Areas with zero cellular signal cannot receive live voice queries (mitigated by offline dealer counter operations).
